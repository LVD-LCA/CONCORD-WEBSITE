About Concord

A long-term approach.
Solid partnerships. Sustainable growth.

Concord for Investments and Development Company Limited was established in Cyprus in 2019. The company operates as a private holding and investment company, pursuing a long-term approach to investments and business development.

Concord partners with entrepreneurs, operating companies and selected projects where active ownership, strategic support and patient capital can contribute to sustainable development.

As the group develops, Concord intends to broaden its portfolio through further participations and strategic partnerships within the European Union.
